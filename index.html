Paint Rumble

A territory game for iPhone and Android. Draw loops, paint your territory and outsmart your rivals.

About the game

Paint Rumble is a game about claiming ground. Leave your home turf, draw a loop and come back to paint everything inside it, but watch out: a rival who touches your trail cuts you off.

  • Free to play, with ads. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase hides the banner.
  • Your progress can be saved to your own iCloud or Google Play Games account.
  • No account or login of ours: just open the game and play.
